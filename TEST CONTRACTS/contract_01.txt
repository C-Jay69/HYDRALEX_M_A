BETWEEN: Buyer Corp ("Buyer")
AND: Seller LLC ("Seller")

**1. EARNOUT FORMULA**

1.1 Earnout Consideration: The earnout shall be based on the target company's EBITDA for the fiscal years ending 2025, 2026, and 2027. The total earnout pool shall not exceed $15,000,000.

1.2 EBITDA Calculation: "EBITDA" shall mean Net Income plus depreciation, amortization, and depletion, further adjusted as follows:
(a) Add back: Stock-based compensation expenses, but only to the extent such expenses exceed 3% of Revenue;
(b) Deduct: Any expenses related to "non-recurring items" as determined solely by Buyer's CFO;
(c) Further adjust: For "one-time" gains or losses from asset sales, regulatory actions, or litigation settlements, as determined by Buyer in good faith.

1.3 Earnout Payment: Based on the EBITDA achieved:
- If EBITDA is at or above 110% of the target, earnout = 100% of pool
- If EBITDA is between 90% and 110% of target, earnout = (EBITDA / Target EBITDA) × pool
- If EBITDA is below 90% of target, earnout = 0

1.4 Dispute Resolution: Any dispute regarding earnout calculation shall be resolved by the parties' respective CFOs, and their decision shall be final and binding.
